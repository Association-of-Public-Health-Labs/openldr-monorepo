## CD4 Count

CD4 cell count testing for immune status monitoring in HIV patients. CD4 counts are critical for identifying patients with advanced HIV disease (CD4 count below 200 cells/mm³) and guiding treatment decisions.

## CrAg (Cryptococcal Antigen)

Cryptococcal antigen testing for diagnosis of cryptococcal meningitis in patients with advanced HIV disease. CrAg screening is recommended for all HIV-positive patients with CD4 counts below 200 cells/mm³.

## TB-LAM (Lateral Flow Urine LAM)

Lateral flow urine lipoarabinomannan (LAM) assay for rapid tuberculosis diagnosis in HIV-positive patients with advanced immunosuppression. TB-LAM testing enables point-of-care TB diagnosis in settings where sputum-based diagnostics are challenging.
